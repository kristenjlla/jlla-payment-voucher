5. Approvals

The voucher requires two appropriate approvers. Verify eligibility with JLLA's approval rules. This generator collects names/positions but does not obtain or verify signatures.
Read official approval rules

Ordinarily obtain Committee Chair and Council VP approvals. If reimbursing the Chair, approvers must be Assoc. Chair, Chair-Elect, VP and/or Executive Committee member. If reimbursing a VP or Board Member, both must be Executive Committee members. If a VP is first approver, second must be an Executive Committee member (President, President-Elect, Treasurer, or Secretary).

6. Review and download

Your packet will include the original one-page JLLA voucher, followed by all attached documents. Finance/Accounting-only fields remain blank. Confirm all supporting documents and required approvals are complete before submitting to your Council Treasurer.

Submit the completed voucher and supporting documentation to your Council Treasurer within 30 days of the expense, per the original instructions.

This independent generator does not submit a payment request or approve reimbursement. Confirm JLLA Finance accepts electronically combined supporting documents and the method of approval/signature.
