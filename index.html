6. Review and download

Your packet will include the filled one-page JLLA voucher, followed by all attached documents. Confirm all supporting documents and required approvals are complete before submitting to your approvers.

Submit the signed voucher and supporting documentation to your Council Treasurer within 30 days of the expense, per JLLA policy.

7. Your PDF has been downloaded — next steps

Keep your downloaded payment voucher and attachments. Downloading the PDF does not submit it to JLLA.
  1. Email the downloaded PDF to your First Approver and Second Approver to request their signatures.
  2. Once both have signed the form and returned it to you, submit the signed file using the “Payment Voucher Submission” form on Member Essentials on the JLLA member website. Include required supporting documents.
  3. Allow 2+ weeks after submission for voucher processing, check cutting, and USPS mailing.

If you have trouble obtaining signatures or completing the form, contact your Council Treasurer, listed under Member Essentials → My League → Councils → Finance.

Ready to request approval? Use the email draft below to contact both approvers. Remember to attach your downloaded PDF before sending. The generator cannot automatically attach files to an email.
